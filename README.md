## About · نبذة

I am a **developer and data analyst** with a frontline background in **Emergency Medical Services & Critical Care** (prehospital care since 2012), an **MSc in Healthcare Leadership & Management**, and a **PhD candidate**. I build data and AI systems for healthcare operations: forecasting models, optimisation engines, evaluation benchmarks and decision dashboards.

**What I build**
- **ML & forecasting**: gradient boosting, time-series models, rolling-origin validation, uncertainty calibration
- **Operations research**: maximal-coverage location models (MCLP / MILP), discrete-event dispatch simulation
- **LLM & agent evaluation**: bilingual Arabic/English benchmarks, hidden graders, cheat-resistant SWE eval tasks
- **Engineering practice**: Python, typed and tested code (pytest), ruff, GitHub Actions CI, Streamlit dashboards

I don't stop at the number: I trace the process that produced it, then ship a tested tool a decision-maker can act on.

> A model is not judged by its accuracy on one split, but by its stability across many validation windows.

### International Air Passengers — Forecasting (1949–1960)
**SDAIA Time Series Course — Capstone Project**

A complete forecasting pipeline: series diagnosis, a Seasonal Naive benchmark floor, model screening through AutoGluon, and an eight-fold rolling-origin cross-validation harness.

**Result:** **AutoARIMA** halves the benchmark's error (MASE 0.66 vs 1.31) — with a full management report and a standalone results page.

<div dir="rtl" align="right">

### تحليل والتنبؤ بركاب الطيران الدولي 1949–1960
**مشروع ختام دورة السلاسل الزمنية — SDAIA**

خط أنابيب كامل لتحليل السلاسل الزمنية: تشخيص السلسلة، تحديد أرضية أساس (Seasonal Naive)، تصفية النماذج عبر AutoGluon، ومحك تدقيق بثماني نوافذ متدحرجة (Rolling-Origin Cross-Validation).

**النتيجة:** نموذج **AutoARIMA** يقلّص خطأ الأرضية إلى النصف (MASE 0.66 مقابل 1.31) — مع تقرير إداري كامل وصفحة عرض مستقلة.

</div>

| Model | MASE ↓ | RMSSE ↓ | Scaled CRPS ↓ | 80% coverage |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **AutoARIMA** — shipped | **0.66** | **0.70** | **0.034** | 0.68 |
| AutoTheta | 0.90 | 0.99 | 0.046 | 0.59 |
| AutoETS | 0.95 | 1.00 | 0.050 | 0.49 |

| Naive — benchmark | 2.12 | 2.45 | 0.110 | 0.69 |

<sub>Averaged over eight rolling-origin folds (12-month horizon, step 12). Each fold is scaled against only the history it was allowed to see. — متوسط ثماني نوافذ متدحرجة بأفق 12 شهراً، وكل نافذة مقيسة على التاريخ المسموح لها به فقط.</sub>
